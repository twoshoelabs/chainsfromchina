Which chains are going global

This tracks which Chinese chains have expanded into selected, key markets outside China.

This page is a register, not a complete count. Entries come from company filings, announcements and the press. Some are compiled by hand and a blank does not ensure that there is no presence in that market. Local census details for countries including Singapore, Japan and the UK are planned.

In each cell: a number = published locations · Y = present, count unpublished · ○ = announced, not yet open · ★ = collected daily by this project · – = searched, none found · · = not looked at · × = entered, then exited. Confidence of a typed entry: ? medium, ?? low; a plain number or Y is high confidence. Hover any cell for the reasoning.
