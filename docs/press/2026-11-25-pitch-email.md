# Media pitch email

*Personalized outreach template for sending the press release to specific reporters. Fill the [bracketed] slots, and send each one individually (no BCC blasts).*

## Subject line (pick one, then personalize)

- China's retail chains now run more than 1,100 US stores
- Data: 89 China-origin chains, 1,100+ US storefronts
- [Outlet] story idea: the Chinese chains hiding in US malls

## Email body

**EMBARGOED until [DATE / TIME ET] (to be set). Please don't publish before then.**

Hi [First name],

[One personalized line, referencing their recent work. e.g. "Your piece on Pop Mart's US expansion is why I'm writing,"]

Chains From China is an independent tracker of China-origin retail and restaurant brands in the US. Its latest count is worth flagging to you: **89 chains now run more than 1,100 US storefronts across 47 states**, the most complete count of the sector to date, and I wanted to offer it under embargo before it goes out.

A few things that might make a story:

- Two brands most shoppers never guess are Chinese, Miniso and Pop Mart, account for about 45% of all those outlets.
- It's expanding and shaking out at once: new chains open almost weekly, while one bubble-tea chain has already closed at least 40 US stores, and at least six chains have left the US altogether.
- The whole thing is browsable as a free interactive map at chainsfromchina.com, filterable by brand and sector.

Happy to walk you through the data, share the underlying numbers, or get on a quick call. The full release is below [and attached as a PDF].

Best, Daniel Silver Retail Analyst, Chains From China (a project of Twoshoe Labs) info@twoshoelabs.com · chainsfromchina.com

## How to use

- Set the embargo date and time before sending, and put that same line at the top of the release too (swap its "FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE" for "EMBARGOED UNTIL [date]").
- For a reporter you don't know, ask first ("Can I send you something under embargo?") rather than assuming they are bound by it.
- Not embargoing? Delete the embargo line and say "Sharing ahead of broader distribution" instead.
- Paste the full press release (the other tab) beneath the signature, and/or attach the PDF.
