    About these numbers

    The record starts on the first day we collected it; we don't backfill from old press reports, so any change we show is only as old as that. The counts are whatever each chain publishes about itself. If a store opens and the chain hasn't updated its locator, we can't see it yet.

    Chains From China is an independent measurement project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by any company named on this site; brand names identify the businesses being measured and nothing more.

    Store counts on the US map are collected daily from each chain's own public store locator. The international register is compiled by hand from company filings, exchange announcements and the trade press. Every row carries its source, its date and how far it should be trusted, and the two are never added together. Coastlines and boundaries from Natural Earth (public domain).
