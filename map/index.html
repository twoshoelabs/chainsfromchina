Where China’s chains are open in America

A daily count of Chinese chains open across the US — bubble tea and hot pot, but also art toys, cosmetics, sportswear and more — taken from each company’s own store list. Every pin is the brand’s own logo; click a cluster to open it, or a pin for the street address. See which have gone global and where they cluster together.

Show on the map: click a brand to toggle it, or a group heading for the whole group

Each pin is a brand’s logo, ringed in its sector color. A filled pin is a confirmed location — an outlet counted daily from the chain’s own locator, or a hand-verified sighting. A hollow, dashed pin is announced / coming-soon, held out of the open total. Clusters show how many outlets fall in an area at the current zoom — click to expand. Prefer tables? The by-state view breaks the count down by state with coverage detail.

The record starts on the first day we collected it; we don’t backfill from old press reports, and the counts are whatever each chain publishes about itself. Basemap: a US Protomaps vector basemap the project self-hosts on static storage — no tile server, no API fees — rendered client-side by open-source MapLibre GL. Chains From China is an independent measurement project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by any company named on this site; brand names identify the businesses being measured and nothing more.

Store counts are collected daily from each chain’s own public store locator; hand-verified sightings are compiled by hand and never added into the daily totals. Brand logos are each company’s own, shown to identify its outlets.
