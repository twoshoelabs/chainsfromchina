New outlets on the way

Beyond the outlets open today, tracked chains have announced — more US locations that haven’t opened yet — new stores from chains already trading here, or, in a few cases, a chain’s first US site. None is counted in the total above until its own store listing shows it open.

A paid tier for people who work with this data

The map and the daily counts are free for anyone to use. Pro adds the full history, alerts, exports and landlord tools, built on the same measured record. It’s meant for landlords and brokers, brand expansion teams, and analysts who track these chains for a living.
