Help fill the map

This project can only see what these chains publish, and most of them publish nothing. If you know a store we're missing, a location that has closed, a chain that has just arrived in the US, or one we should be following, tell us. Openings and closings both help. Submit a source or data point

A sourced fact about a chain we track — a store count, a filing figure, a corporate detail, a piece of history — with where it came from. This feeds the project's deeper intelligence layer. Everything is checked and keeps its source; we never publish a number without one.

Submissions go to the project for review; nothing you send is published automatically. The test is China origin. Chains that only sell Chinese products, or that come from Hong Kong, are outside this project's scope.
