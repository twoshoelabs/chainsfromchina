Vector map beta

The same stores and sightings as the US store map, on a zoomable vector basemap with clustering. Pan and zoom; click a cluster to expand it, a pin for detail.

Pins are colored by sector. A filled pin is an open outlet; a hollow pin is announced / coming-soon or a reported-but-unconfirmed sighting, held out of totals. Clusters show how many outlets fall in an area at the current zoom.

Basemap: a clean light style, rendered client-side by open-source MapLibre GL — no tile-server or API fees. The project plans to self-host a US Protomaps (PMTiles) basemap on static storage for the production version; the basemap here is a one-line swap away from that.
