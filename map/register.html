Which chains are going global

A chain that has crossed one border tends to cross others. This tracks which mainland-China-origin chains have expanded outside China — and which of them have already reached the United States.

This page is a register, not a census. Everything on the US map is something the collector saw for itself and can show a raw capture for. Nothing here is: these are facts from company filings, exchange announcements and the trade press, typed in by hand, each carrying the date it was true and how far it should be trusted. A count that has not been published is left blank rather than estimated, and “no evidence” means a search found nothing — which is a statement about the search, not about the chain.

Keeping it honest

Entries carry a review date and go stale; register --stale lists anything untouched for 90 days. The failure mode of a hand-kept register is not being wrong the day it is written — it is being believed a year later.

Chains From China is an independent measurement project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by any company named on this site; brand names identify the businesses being measured and nothing more.

Store counts on the US map are collected daily from each chain's own public store locator. The international register is compiled by hand from company filings, exchange announcements and the trade press, and every row carries its source, its date and how far it should be trusted — the two are never added together.
