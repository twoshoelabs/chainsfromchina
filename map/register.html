Which chains are going global

This tracks which Chinese chains have expanded into selected, key markets outside China.

This page is a register, not a complete count. Entries come from company filings, announcements and the press. Some are compiled by hand and a blank does not ensure that there is no presence in that market. Local census details for countries including Singapore, Japan and the UK are planned.

In each cell: a number = published locations · Y = present, count unpublished · ★ = collected daily by this project · – = searched, none found · · = not looked at · × = entered, then exited. Confidence of a typed entry: ? medium, ?? low; a plain number or Y is high confidence. Hover any cell for the reasoning.

Chains From China is an independent commercial project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by any company named on this site. Brand names serve to identify the businesses being measured only.

Store counts on the US map are collected daily from each chain's own public store locator. The international register is compiled by hand from company filings, exchange announcements and the trade press, and every row carries its source, its date and how far it should be trusted — the two are never added together.
