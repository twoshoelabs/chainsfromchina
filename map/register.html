Which chains are going global

This tracks which Chinese chains have expanded outside China, and which of them have reached the United States.

This page is a register, not a census. Everything on the US map is something the collector saw for itself and can show a raw capture for. This page is different. The entries come from company filings, exchange announcements and the trade press, compiled by hand, each with the date it was true and a note on how far to trust it. Where a count has not been published we leave it blank instead of guessing. “No evidence” means a search turned up nothing; it does not mean the chain is absent.

In each cell: a number = published locations · Y = present, count unpublished · ★ = collected daily by this project · – = searched, none found · · = not looked at. Confidence of a typed entry: ? medium, ?? low; a plain number or Y is high confidence. Hover any cell for the reasoning.

Keeping it honest

Entries carry a review date and go stale. register --stale lists anything untouched for 90 days, so an old entry can be found and re-checked before anyone relies on it.

Chains From China is an independent measurement project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by any company named on this site; brand names identify the businesses being measured and nothing more.

Store counts on the US map are collected daily from each chain's own public store locator. The international register is compiled by hand from company filings, exchange announcements and the trade press, and every row carries its source, its date and how far it should be trusted — the two are never added together.
