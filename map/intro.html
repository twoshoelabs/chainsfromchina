Who these companies are

A short introduction, for US readers, to each China-origin chain this project follows — with a link to the company's own site in China and its US site where one exists.

A note on the links. Links to a company's own site in China are provided for reference and are untested by this project — they may be in Chinese, may behave differently outside China, and are followed at your own discretion. US sites are the ones to use for ordering or store hours.

Chains From China is an independent measurement project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by any company named on this site; brand names identify the businesses being measured and nothing more.
