Where Chinese chains cluster

Places where two or more China-origin chains trade in the same building, mall or block.

This is computed, not compiled. Every store and sighting on this site is geocoded, so a cluster here is simply chains sitting at the same coordinates — the same building, mall or block. It needs no mall directory: the evidence is the stores themselves, each of which you can find on the US map. Where we know the center by name and its owner, that is shown; otherwise the cluster stands on its location alone. Only clusters with two or more distinct brands are listed.

Chains From China is an independent measurement project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by any company named on this site; brand names identify the businesses being measured and nothing more.

Clusters are computed from the geocoded coordinates of stores this project collects daily and of hand-verified sightings; two chains within about 100 m are treated as sharing a center. Coordinates are derived from the US Census geocoder. Coastlines from Natural Earth (public domain).
