Terms & data use

What is free to view and cite, and the limits on bulk use of the dataset.

Last updated 17 November 2026

Summary

Chains From China is a project of Twoshoe Labs LLC. The interactive map and the headline counts are free to view and free to cite with attribution. What is not permitted is taking the underlying dataset in bulk — scraping it, redistributing it, or republishing it as your own. The paid Pro layer is licensed separately. The plain-language version is below; it governs your use of this site and its data.

What is free

  • Viewing the map, the chain pages, and the counts, for any purpose.
  • Quoting or citing the figures and findings in reporting, research, or commentary, with attribution to Chains From China (chainsfromchina.com).
  • Linking to the site and to individual chain pages.

If you cite a figure, please note that it is a point-in-time count that updates daily, and link back so readers can see the live number.

What is not permitted

Without written permission, you may not:

  • Extract, scrape, or download the dataset in bulk, whether by automated means or by hand, in whole or in substantial part. This includes the JSON and GeoJSON data files and the content of the chain pages.
  • Redistribute, resell, sublicense, or republish the data, or incorporate it into another product, dataset, or service.
  • Use the data to build or train a competing database, directory, or model of the same subject.
  • Remove, obscure, or alter the attribution, license, or provenance information carried in the data or on the pages.

Pro data

Revenue, capacity, locator-freshness, and comparative home-market estimates, together with the sources behind them, are part of a separately licensed Pro tier. They are not included in the public map or the public data files, and nothing on this page grants a license to them.

Provenance and enforcement

The public data carries an embedded license notice and a per-snapshot fingerprint, and the project maintains its own tamper-evident record of how the count was built. Individual records carry quiet, documented markers. Copies of the dataset are therefore traceable to their source and date. Where the data is taken or republished in breach of these terms, Twoshoe Labs will pursue the remedies available to it, including takedown requests and claims under applicable law.

No warranty

The site is in beta. The data is compiled on a best-effort basis and is provided “as is,” without warranty of any kind. Counts are a deliberately conservative floor and will change as coverage deepens and stores open and close. Do not rely on the figures for decisions without independent verification. Brand names are used only to identify the businesses being tracked; the project is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any company it measures.
